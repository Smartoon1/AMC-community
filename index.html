The Meaning

What is a Mobilator?

A Mobilator A Mobilador (or Mobilator) is a mobile gaming setup that allows you to play your favorite smartphone games using a physical keyboard and mouse. By bridging the gap between mobile hardware and PC-style controls, it gives players precise aiming, faster reaction times, and custom key bindings on games that usually rely solely on touch controls.

Coined by AMC, the term celebrates the new generation of African esports athletes competing across mobile titles like Free Fire, Blood Strike, and CODM. A Mobilator doesn't wait for a high-end console or PC; they mobilize with the gear in their hands, turning raw passion into a profession and transforming smartphones into battle arenas.

Every Mobilator carries three values everywhere they go:

  • ✦ Play — compete with skill, discipline and respect.
  • ✦ Connect — build friendships and networks across borders.
  • ✦ Grow — level up in-game and in life, together.

PLAY

Competitive mobilator gaming at every level — from casual community nights to ranked tournaments with real stakes.

CONNECT

GROW

Coaching, content creation and career pathways — AMC turns gaming talent into sustainable opportunity.

The Journey

The Story Behind AMC

Born from a vision in Accra, the African Mobilator Community exists to unlock the potential of mobile gamers across the continent.

Born from a vision in Nigeria, the African Mobilator Community exists to unlock the potential of mobile gamers across the continent. AMC started with two friends both passionate mobilator players grinding ranked matches after schools. No fancy setups, just phones, adapters, keyboards, mouse, passion, and a dream. What began in a single gaming corner in Nigeria has grown into a movement spanning multiple African countries, connecting players who refuse to let equipment decide their destiny.

Today, AMC runs community tournaments, training sessions, content collabs, and mentorship programs—building a pipeline from local gaming hubs to the main stages of African esports. Our mission is simple: every mobilator gamer deserves a community, a coach, and a chance to compete.

Our Mission

To unite, train and showcase Africa's mobilator gaming talent to the world.

Our Vision

A continent where every Mobilator can play professionally and profitably.

The Visionary

Meet the Founder

FOUNDER

SMARTOON — "Embracing the Digital Future"

Banire Abdulquawiyu who is known as Smartoon, an innovator and esports enthusiast, founded AMC to give mobile gamers something the continent had never had — a home.

Growing up in Nigeria, Smartoon watched brilliant young players dominate mobile lobbies with nothing but talent and touchscreens—yet remain invisible to the wider esports world. Recognized as the top Free Fire mobilator player in Nigeria, he knew firsthand that the phone in your pocket was never a limitation; it was a launchpad.

Driven by that belief, Smartoon founded the African Mobilator Community in December 2025. Under his leadership, AMC has grown from a humble vision into a structured movement complete with competitive roles, community events, and strategic partnerships—proving that African mobile gaming deserves a seat at the global table.

  • ✦ Founded AMC in Nigeria, South Africa
  • ✦ Architect of the "Mobilator" identity & player roles
  • ✦ Leads AMC tournaments & partnerships

COMMUNITY LEADER

SILENT MOBILATOR — "Fostering Connection"

OYEKANMI BOLUWATIFE TAIWO who is known as Silent has been a cornerstone of AMC since day one standing alongside Smartoon when the vision was first born. He is the heart and energy that keeps the community alive, structured, and constantly growing.

A natural leader with an unmatched ability to unite players, Silent turned a passion for mobile gaming into a thriving movement. His philosophy is simple: communities don't grow by numbers, they grow by connection and representation. Every squad alignment, community highlight, and viral moment carries his signature drive to elevate African mobile esports.
